## Pre-deployment checklist

Complete this checklist before leaving the office for a field deployment:

- Confirm 4G signal strength at the target mount height is ≥ −90 dBm (test with a phone
  on the same carrier before ordering a site visit)
- Confirm a power source (mains socket or PoE) within 10 m of the target mount location
- Record the node serial number, IMEI, and provisioned SIM ICCID in the deployment log
- Download the node firmware package for the model being deployed
- Bring a spare SIM from the provisioned pool in case the primary SIM has a fault
- Confirm the MQTT broker endpoint, credentials, and topic prefix are ready to enter on site

## Mounting the enclosure

The IP65 enclosure is designed for outdoor vertical-surface mounting.

**Orientation**: mount vertically with the cable glands facing downward. This ensures water
drains away from the gland seals. Horizontal mounting is not supported.

**Antenna position**: the LTE antenna is internal. The enclosure must have a clear sky view or
be oriented toward an open window. Avoid mounting:

- Behind reinforced concrete walls or metal panels
- Inside a metal cabinet or meter box
- Adjacent to large metal HVAC or conduit runs

**Fasteners**: use M6 stainless bolts with nylon washers. The mounting plate is pre-drilled — match the bolt pattern and torque to the figures printed on the plate label. Do not over-tighten — the ABS body will crack.

## SIM and connectivity configuration

1. Open the enclosure using the captive M4 screws (included hex key required).
2. Insert the provisioned SIM card into the LTE slot. The card orientation is marked on the SIM
   holder — gold contacts facing down, notch facing left.
3. Close the enclosure and secure the lid screws to the torque specified on the lid label.
4. Connect power.

## MQTT endpoint configuration

After the unit is online, connect to the provisioning access point to configure the cloud endpoint:

1. On a phone or laptop, connect to the Wi-Fi SSID: `ARIOT-NODE-<serial>` using the default
   password printed on the label inside the lid.
2. Open a browser and navigate to `192.168.4.1`.
3. Navigate to **Settings → Cloud → MQTT**.
4. Enter:
   - **Broker**: the endpoint provided in your provisioning portal
   - **Port**: as specified in the provisioning portal (TLS recommended)
   - **Username**: the credential issued with your provisioning pack
   - **Password**: from the provisioning portal
   - **Topic prefix**: the prefix assigned to your deployment
5. Tap **Save and restart**. The node will reboot and connect to the broker within 30 seconds.
6. Verify by checking the broker topic in the monitoring dashboard — you should see a heartbeat
   message within 60 seconds of restart.
