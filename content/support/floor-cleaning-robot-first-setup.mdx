## Unboxing checklist

Before powering on, confirm all items are present in the box:

- Robot unit × 1
- Docking station × 1
- Power adapter (check the rating label matches your outlet voltage before connecting)
- Cleaning pad × 2
- Quick-start card × 1

If any item is missing or the robot shows visible shipping damage, contact us through the Contact page before proceeding. Do not attempt to power on a physically
damaged unit.

## Docking station placement

The docking station placement is critical for reliable autonomous operation. Follow these rules:

- **Wall clearance**: place the station against a flat, straight wall with no objects within
  50 cm on either side.
- **Front clearance**: keep at least 150 cm of clear floor in front of the dock. The robot
  approaches at an angle and needs this space to align correctly.
- **Cable management**: the dock power cable must not cross the robot's return path. Route it
  behind the dock or along the wall using the included cable clip.
- **Lighting**: the dock works with the front infrared beacon. Avoid placing it directly under
  a strong downlight that may interfere with IR reception.

Connect the power adapter to the dock and to a wall outlet. The indicator light should turn steady within 10 seconds.

## Wi-Fi setup

Download the companion app from the App Store or Google Play — store links are published alongside product availability.

1. Open the app and create an account (or sign in if you have one).
2. Tap **Add device** and select **Floor-cleaning robot**.
3. Ensure your phone is connected to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. **Note**: 5 GHz networks are not
   supported in this firmware version.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts. The robot will enter pairing mode automatically when powered on
   for the first time.
5. Enter your Wi-Fi password when prompted. The robot will connect and you will see it appear in
   the app device list within 60 seconds.

If pairing fails, see the Wi-Fi pairing article in the support hub.

## First mapping clean

The first clean run maps the space. This run takes longer than subsequent cleans — typically 20–30
minutes for a medium-sized room. During this run:

- **Do not move furniture** — mid-map changes will corrupt the map and require a remap.
- **Keep the room clear of people and pets** where possible — moving obstacles during mapping
  reduce map quality.
- **Leave internal doors open** if you want the robot to map connecting rooms in one run.

To start the first mapping clean:

1. Place the robot on the docking station. Wait for the charge indicator to show at least 40%.
2. Press the **Clean** button on the robot, or tap **Start clean** in the app.
3. The robot will leave the dock and begin a systematic sweep.

After the first run, the map is saved to the app. Subsequent cleans use the saved map for
navigation and are significantly faster.
